Multivariate Market Regime Shifts via Rolling PCA & Clustering

2025

Processed high-frequency 2-minute tick returns across 480 S&P 500 constituents to model time-varying microstructure. Implemented a rolling 5-day Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to extract latent market factors, utilizing eigenvalue loadings as dynamic features for K-means clustering.

Successfully identified discrete "risk-on" vs. "risk-off" macroeconomic regimes with 91% statistical confidence, validating state transitions against SPY and VXX index volatility overlays.
