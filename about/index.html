About Me

Dominicus Johan Nararya • Auckland, New Zealand • Data Science & Complex Systems

I am a postgraduate data science researcher at the University of Auckland, specializing in statistical modeling, time-series forecasting, anomaly detection, and complex adaptive systems.

Research Philosophy & Complex Systems

Traditional deterministic models often fail when confronted with non-linear feedback loops and sudden regime changes. I am fascinated by how collective intelligence arises without central coordination. The background simulation on this website illustrates Craig Reynolds’ Boids model—a three-rule local interaction system (Separation, Alignment, Cohesion) that spontaneously gives rise to the fluid, protective murmuration of pelagic fish schools.

“The whole is not only more than the sum of its parts, but also fundamentally different in kind.”
— Philip W. Anderson, More Is Different (1972)

Experience & Background

At Defence, Science and Technology (DST) New Zealand, I engineered spatial-temporal tracking algorithms that reconstructed vessel trajectories with 98% accuracy across challenging inshore and offshore AIS gaps, alongside automated schema verification systems for maritime surveillance.

Beyond research, I serve as a part-time reservist in the New Zealand Defence Force, cultivating high-pressure decision making, rigorous precision, and cross-functional team leadership.
