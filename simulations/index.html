Emergent Phenomena to Observe

  1. Information Shockwaves: When you quickly flick the cursor through a cluster, only the nearest fish directly perceive the predator. However, their sudden evasive acceleration breaks alignment with neighbors, triggering a visible cascade that sweeps across the entire school.
  2. Vortical Swirls & Gyres: When boundary steering balances with cohesion, fish naturally transition into rotating toruses (baitballs), minimizing perimeter exposure.
